Privacy Policy — LevelBook by MvP

Effective Date: November 23, 2026  •  Package ID: com.mvplabs.levelbook  •  Developer: MvP Labs

1. Overview

LevelBook by MvP is an offline-first civil and geomatic differential leveling survey field book and report generator. Your survey observations, client details, and firm branding remain exclusively on your device under your control.

2. Zero Data Collection & Zero Permissions

LevelBook does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal or usage data to MvP Labs or any third party.

  • Zero Permissions Required: LevelBook declares zero Android manifest or runtime permissions (no internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or broad storage permissions).
  • On-Device Sandbox Storage: All survey jobs, station readings, benchmark elevations, client metadata, and firm profile settings are stored strictly inside your device's private application storage.
  • System Photo Picker for Optional Logos: Selecting a company logo uses Android's built-in Photo Picker, granting single-file read access only to the image you explicitly pick.

3. Device Backup & One-Tap Data Deletion

  • If Android System Backup / Device Transfer is enabled on your phone, Android may include your local survey files in your personal OS device backup. MvP Labs cannot view or access these backups.
  • You can delete individual field books at any time from Saved Survey Level Books, wipe all local jobs, client info, and logos in one tap via About & Privacy Info → Clear All Saved Jobs, Client Details & Logos, or uninstall the app.

4. User-Initiated PDF & CSV Exports

Editable AcroForm PDF reports and UTF-8 CSV field books are generated locally on your device and are only exported when you explicitly tap Share or Save File using Android's system Sharesheet or Storage Access Framework.

5. No Analytics, Trackers, or Advertising

LevelBook embeds zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero crash/telemetry trackers, and zero advertising SDKs.

6. Children's Privacy

LevelBook collects zero personal information from users of any age, including children under 13.
